EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Entertainment tracking apps like Letterboxd and MyDramaList demonstrate strong engagement potential with DAU/MAU ratios estimated at 15-25% for healthy platforms. Letterboxd reached 17-26 million registered users with a freemium model ($19/year Pro, $49/year Patron). K-drama enthusiasts track 5+ shows monthly, with episode-based tracking being critical for serialized content. Korean content exports reached $561M in 2022 (30% YoY growth), driven by Netflix's $2.5B investment. MyDramaList shows 5,000+ titles in top results with rich metadata including episode counts, airing status, networks, and content ratings.

## Essential K-Drama Metadata Fields

Based on MyDramaList and industry standards, the following fields are critical for K-drama tracking:

### Core Identification
- Native title (Korean)
- English title
- Alternate titles (romanization variants)
- Country of origin
- Type (Drama, Movie, Special, Variety Show)

### Production Information
- Director(s)
- Screenwriter(s)
- Production company
- Original network (KBS, MBC, SBS, tvN, Netflix, Disney+, etc.)
- Airing dates (start and end)
- Airing schedule (day of week)

### Content Structure
- Episode count
- Episode duration
- Season information (for multi-season shows)
- Airing status (Currently Airing, Finished Airing, Not Yet Aired)

### Classification
- Genres (see taxonomy below)
- Tags (community-driven themes)
- Content rating (age-based)
- Official synopsis

### Engagement Metrics
- User score (community-rated)
- Popularity rank
- Watcher count
- Review count

## Letterboxd Pricing Model

### Tier Structure
| Tier | Price | Features |
|------|-------|----------|
| Free | $0 | Core tracking, reviews, lists |
| Pro | $19/year | Stats, ad-free, streaming filters, notifications |
| Patron | $49/year | Custom artwork, early access, recognition |

### Monetization Strategy
1. **Freemium model** - Free tier for growth, paid for monetization
2. **Low friction upgrade** - $19/year is ~$1.58/month
3. **Value-add features** - Stats, filters, customization
4. **Recognition tier** - Patron appeals to super-fans

### Gated Features
- Ad-free experience
- Personalized statistics
- Streaming availability filters
- Watchlist notifications
- Custom posters/backdrops (Patron)
- Early beta access (Patron)
